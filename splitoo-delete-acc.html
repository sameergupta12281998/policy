Account & Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account

To permanently delete your Splitoo account and all associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Open the Splitoo app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile icon at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Your account will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your request.

Can't Access the App?

If you are unable to access the app, you can submit a deletion request by emailing us directly:

Subject: Account Deletion Request
Include: The email address associated with your Splitoo account.

Email Us to Delete Account

What Data Is Deleted or Retained

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please review what is deleted and what may be retained below.
Data Type Action Retention Period
Profile (name, avatar, bio) Deleted Within 30 days
Email address Deleted Within 30 days
Groups you created Deleted Within 30 days
Expenses you added Deleted Within 30 days
Settlement records Deleted Within 30 days
Authentication tokens Deleted Immediately
Anonymous crash / diagnostic logs Retained Up to 90 days (no personal data)
