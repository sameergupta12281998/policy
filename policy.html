Privacy Policy for Vibetra

Effective date: April 6, 2026

Vibetra ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Vibetra app as a free application. This page explains how information is handled when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Vibetra itself does not directly collect or store personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

However, the app uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information, including:

  • Device information (e.g., device model, operating system)
  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and general usage data
  • App interaction data (for analytics and ad delivery)

This data is collected by third-party providers and is not stored or controlled by Vibetra.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob – Used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data for ad delivery and personalization.
  • Audius – Music streaming platform used via public API.
  • Jamendo – Creative Commons music catalog accessed via public API.
  • YouTube Data API – Used for music search metadata only. No audio is streamed from YouTube.
  • YouTube API Services Terms

These services may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies. Vibetra does not control or access this data directly.

Data Usage

Any data collected by third-party services is used for:

  • Displaying advertisements
  • Improving app performance and stability
  • Providing music search and streaming functionality

Vibetra does not sell, trade, or share user data with third parties directly.

Advertising

We use Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, and app interaction data.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

For more details, visit: Google Ads Policy

Internet Access

The app requires an internet connection to stream music and display advertisements. Vibetra does not store listening history on external servers. Any preferences or history are stored locally on your device.

Copyright and Licensing

Music, artwork, and metadata displayed in Vibetra are provided by third-party services and remain the property of their respective owners and licensors.

Vibetra does not host or upload copyrighted content. Playback is limited to streams made available by content providers.

Trademark and Affiliation Disclaimer

Vibetra is an independent application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Google, YouTube, Spotify, Audius, or Jamendo.

Children's Privacy

Vibetra is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the app. However, since data collection is handled by third-party services, users should review their respective privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted on this page.
